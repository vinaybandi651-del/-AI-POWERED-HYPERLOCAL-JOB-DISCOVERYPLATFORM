How AI Matches Students With Jobs

An explainable, multi-factor recommendation engine combining TF-IDF Content Similarity, Haversine Geo-Proximity, and Collaborative Interaction Matrices.

The End-to-End AI Matching Architecture Pipeline

1

Student Profile Feature Extraction

Parses the student's skills list, degree program, course schedule window (days & hours), GPS latitude/longitude, and minimum compensation expectations.

↓
2

Content-Based Filtering (TF-IDF & Jaccard Overlap)

Constructs term-frequency inverse-document-frequency (TF-IDF) feature vectors of job descriptions and student bio. Evaluates direct required skill overlap and category fit.

↓
3

Location-Aware Matching (Haversine Formula)

Calculates exact great-circle distance \(d = 2r \arcsin(\sqrt{\sin^2(\Delta\phi/2) + \cos\phi_1\cos\phi_2\sin^2(\Delta\lambda/2)})\). Applies smooth exponential decay penalties beyond the student's maximum travel limit.

↓
4

Collaborative Filtering (Interaction History Matrix)

Computes Item-to-Item and User-to-User cosine similarity from the weighted student-job interaction matrix (View=1, Search=2, Save=3, Apply=5, Accept=7, Complete=10).

↓
5

Weighted Hybrid Ensemble & Transparent Match Score

Normalizes scores into a 0–100% rating:
Final Score = (Content × 0.40) + (Location × 0.25) + (Availability × 0.15) + (Collaborative × 0.10) + (Salary × 0.10)
