How It Works

An intelligent bridge connecting students with flexible part-time gigs and local shops needing fast reliable help.

📍

1. Hyperlocal Geo-Detection

Specify your campus hostel, home coordinates, or allow browser GPS. The Haversine algorithm continuously ranks jobs within 1–10 km of where you actually are.

🧠

2. Hybrid AI Recommendation

Content-based TF-IDF compares your skills and coursework, while collaborative filtering identifies roles your peer students engaged with and succeeded at.

💬

3. Instant Apply & Chat

1-click application pipeline with cover notes, direct one-to-one messaging with store managers, and transparent stage tracking from review to completion.
